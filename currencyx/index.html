CurrencyX: Converter & Rates

Your money abroad, clear in one second.

DownloadApp Store

CurrencyX shows live exchange rates for 150+ currencies, tells you how much an exchange booth or card really charges, reads receipts and menus from a photo, and keeps your trip spending in your own currency.

What you get

Convert many currencies at once, with a built-in calculator

Fee check

Enter the booth or card rate and see the hidden fee and the money you lose

Card check

Paying by card abroad? See if “pay in your currency” costs more, and how much you save in local currency

Home Screen widget with your live rates

Share your trip as a clean image card

Scan prices

Photograph a menu or price tag and see every price in your currency

Scan receipts

The amount, currency and shop are filled in for you to check

Trip journal

Budget, today, per day and spending by category, in your home currency

Rate alerts and charts up to 10 years

Works offline with the last rates

12 languages, including Arabic

Pricing

Free: converter, fee check, 1 trip and 3 scans per month. CurrencyX PRO: no ads, all currencies with crypto and gold, rate alerts, 10-year charts, unlimited trips with CSV export and 60 scans per month.

CurrencyX PRO is an auto-renewing subscription (monthly or yearly; a free trial may be offered). Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm. It renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings › Apple ID › Subscriptions.
