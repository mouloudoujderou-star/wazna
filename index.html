How it works

Three taps from plate to log

Scan, check, save. You stay in control of every number.

1

Scan your dish

Point the camera at your plate. Keefa estimates calories, protein, carbs and fat.

FAQ

Questions, answered

How accurate are the numbers?

Reference dishes come from a table of typical recipes. Anything else is an estimate and is labelled as one. Portions vary, so check the grams before you save.

Do I need an account?

No. You can use Keefa without one. Create an account only if you want a cloud backup of your profile, meals and fasting history.

Is my meal photo stored?

No. The photo is only used to read the dish. Barcode scans send only the number to the Open Food Facts database. Read the privacy policy for the full details.

Which languages does it support?

English, Arabic (right to left), French, Spanish, Portuguese, German, Italian, Turkish, Indonesian, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Hindi, Vietnamese, Thai, Polish and Dutch.

When is it available?

Keefa is coming soon to the App Store for iPhone. Check back here for the link.

Is Keefa a medical app?

No. Keefa gives estimates for general information and is not a medical device. Talk to a doctor before you change how you eat or fast.
