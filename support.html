Support

Questions, problems, or a data request: write to support@keefa.app. Please include your phone model, iOS version and what happened. We read every message.

Common questions

A scan was wrong. Tap the calories of an item to type your own number, or change the weight. Keep "Remember my numbers" on and the app uses your version next time.

How do I cancel my subscription? In the iPhone Settings app: your name, Subscriptions, Keefa, Cancel. Do it at least 24 hours before the renewal date.

How do I delete my data? If you created an account: Settings, Cloud backup, Delete account. This deletes your cloud data at once. Data kept only on your phone is removed when you delete the app.
