Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Keefa ("we") helps you log meals and fasting. This page says exactly what the app does with your information. We keep it short on purpose.

What stays on your phone

Your profile (age, sex, height, weight, goal), your meals, your fasting, your settings and your saved dishes are stored on your phone. Without an account, they stay there.

Meal photos and AI

When you scan a meal, the photo and your app language are sent over an encrypted connection to our server, and from there to a third-party AI service provider, which reads the dish and estimates calories and macros. We ask for your permission before the first scan. We do not store your photo on our server after the result comes back. The provider processes the photo under its commercial API terms. Only the photo and your app language go on to the provider. We never pass your name, email, weight or other profile details to it. You can always add meals by hand without AI. If you want to know which provider we use, write to oujderrou@icloud.com and we will tell you.

Barcode scanning

When you scan a barcode on packaged food, the barcode number is sent from your phone over an encrypted connection to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a public product database written by its community, to read the product's nutrition. Nothing else is sent with it: no photo, name, email or profile details. Open Food Facts can see your internet address, as any website can, and handles it under its own privacy policy. We do not use AI for barcode scans and they do not use your scan allowance. Products you have looked up are kept on your phone so a repeat scan works without a signal. Product data comes from Open Food Facts contributors under the Open Database Licence. It can contain mistakes, so check it against the label.

Scan limits and a device identifier

To apply the plan's scan limits we keep, for each user, the time of each successful scan and a random identifier created when you install the app. The app also creates an anonymous account automatically so limits belong to a person. It has no email and no name.

Optional account and cloud backup

If you create an account (email and password, or Sign in with Apple), we store your email, your name if you give one, and a backup of your profile, targets, meals, fasting preferences, fasting history, weigh-ins and saved dishes with our database provider, Supabase. Meal photos are not part of the backup. You can delete your account in the app (Settings, Cloud backup, Delete account): everything in the cloud is deleted at once.

Location

Only if you turn on sunrise-to-sunset fasting, the app reads your approximate location once to work out suhoor and iftar times on your phone. The location stays on your phone and is never sent to us.

Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple. We use RevenueCat to check whether a subscription is active; it receives purchase status linked to your anonymous or account identifier. We never see your card.

Notifications

Reminders (fasting alerts, suhoor and iftar) are created on your phone. We do not send marketing notifications.

What we do not do

We do not show ads, we do not track you across other apps or websites, we do not sell your data, and the app contains no advertising or analytics software.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or take a copy, by writing to oujderrou@icloud.com. Deleting your account in the app does the deletion for you.

Children

Keefa is not for children under 14, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Health information

Calorie and macro numbers are estimates for general information. They are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor or dietitian about your diet, especially if you are pregnant, under 18, or have a medical condition.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, we update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app. Contact: oujderrou@icloud.com.
